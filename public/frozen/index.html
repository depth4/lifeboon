Lifeboon

A city-scale life simulator that runs on real map data. Streets, building footprints, heights, parks and water come from OpenStreetMap; the people, their jobs and their days are simulated on top of that geometry.

Controls

  • Drag orbit · Right-drag or Shift+drag pan
  • Wheel zoom — continuous from 3 km up to eye level
  • WASD move · Shift faster
  • Click a person to inspect them, then Follow
  • Click a building for what the map says about it
  • Drive puts a car on the nearest street below you

Driving

  • W accelerate · S brake, then reverse · AD steer
  • R reverse gear · Space handbrake · Esc get out
  • Drag look around · Wheel move the camera in or out

The car is simulated from a published specification: mass, power, torque, gear ratios, final drive, drag and rolling resistance. It accelerates, changes gear, struggles on hills and stops in the distance those numbers imply, and it loses grip in a corner when the speed asks for more than the tyres can give. It does not yet slide, have suspension, or hit anything except a building wall — and there is no other traffic to hit.

What is deliberately not here

  • No interiors. Buildings are closed shells. When somebody is at home or at work they are simply not drawn.
  • No brands. Shop, cafe and business names are discarded on import; only the category is kept, so a cafe is "a cafe".
  • No real people. Inhabitants are invented and statistical. Nothing is tied to who actually lives at an address.

Send this place to the developer

Overpass cannot be reached from the sandbox this is built in, so a real town can only be tested if it is carried there by hand. This saves the loaded area — streets, buildings, land cover and the terrain under them — as one file you can attach to a message. Load one back to see exactly what somebody else was looking at.
